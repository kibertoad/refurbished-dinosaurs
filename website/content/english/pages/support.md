The restorations are done by people working with AI agents, and the agents cost money to run. You can support the project through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/kibertoad).

Donations pay for model tokens and for the cloud machines the agents run on. Every 150 EUR donated lets us work on one more game in parallel with the ones already under way. The [games page](/games/) shows what is being restored now, and the [wishlist](/wishlist/) shows what people would like to see next.
